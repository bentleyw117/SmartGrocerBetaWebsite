Privacy

This page is a short draft for human approval. It is not legal advice.

Last updated August 17, 2026.

Who we are

Smart Grocer is a US beta mobile app. This website collects a waitlist so we can invite testers. Questions: hello@smartgrocerapp.com.

What we collect on this site

When you request beta access, we store:

  • Email (required)
  • First name, if you share it
  • 5-digit US zip, if you share it
  • Where you usually shop, if you share it
  • The date you joined, and a browser user-agent string

We do not ask for payment, an Apple ID, a grocery list, or a password on this page.

How we use it

We only use this to invite you to the Smart Grocer beta. Zip and usual stores help us understand coverage and invite the right testers first. They are not a promise that a particular store is supported.

After you join, we send a confirmation email from Smart Grocer <hello@smartgrocerapp.com>. We do not sell this list.

Where it lives

Waitlist rows are stored in our database so we can export testers. Access is limited to operating the beta. We do not publish the list.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist records while the beta is active, then delete or archive them when they are no longer needed to invite testers.

Your choices

Email hello@smartgrocerapp.com to correct your details or ask us to remove you from the waitlist.

This page

The public site is https://beta.smartgrocerapp.com. This privacy draft covers the waitlist form on that site. The mobile app, if you later receive access, may have its own notice.
