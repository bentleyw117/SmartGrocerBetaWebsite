The unpaid homework

You already know the stores. The work is checking them.

Open a grocer’s site. Search a product. Compare pack sizes. Repeat for every item, on every site you already use. Then you still have to drive. Smart Grocer does that homework, then hands you a trip you shop in the aisle.

How a trip works

List. Optimize. Shop.

Type a grocery list, one item per line. We find live local products and prices, pick the lowest matching price per item, and group the result into a trip across your usual stores. Unmatched items are dropped, not guessed.

01 · List

Write what you need

One item per line. Diet preferences can influence matching.

02 · Optimize

We do the comparison

Lowest matching price per item, then grouped by store. We'll prefer products that match your diet when we optimize your trip.

03 · Trip

See where to go

Which of your usual stores to hit, which items, estimated total before tax. We'll prioritize prices from your favorites.

04 · Shop

Walk the aisle

Maps plus an in-store checklist. You pay at the store, like always.
